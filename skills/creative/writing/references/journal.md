# 週記

Load for 週記 / 日記 / dated life log. Spoken 粵語, dated, objects first.

Sources: 2026 dated pieces (九月中, 9月初, 8月底, 好野我得左啦, 卡卡頌香港2026政變) plus restart note in `貓寶寶走了` / `十年後重啟寫作`.

## Job

Record the week so future-Helic (or an agent) can see objects, people-as-roles, and the judgment that grew out of them. Publish channel is secondary.

## Cadence

Spoken 粵語: 喺、嘅、咗、真係、好似、咪、嗰啲. Medium-long. Jump from AI theory to 地鐵去觀塘 is correct, not a flaw.

Date the entry. Number next-steps. End in a concrete 下一步, not a slogan.

## Moves

- Start with where the body was (屋企、office、酒樓、密室、巴士死火).
- Mine talk for a usable rule, then caveat: 「我自己唔係好肯定係咪啱」.
- First-glance vs second-glance on AI output.
- 證明自己: 小而能跑 (dashboard、小遊戲), 唔好大平台.
- Grief/work: 返 office 當 distraction 可以寫, 唔好寫成雞湯.

## Anti

- Leaking real colleague/family names into a shareable draft. Use roles such as 同事、朋友、家人, 唔好當人事檔.
- 小說「明顯係」判詞堆疊.
- 散文 行文日期／密室 SHB117 儀式 — 週記用普通日期即可.

## Example

今個星期三同星期四都留喺屋企搵工, 上午先研究 grok. 成品唔顯著, 但比用 AI 寫小說有更大 value. 一諗到花時間教屋企 AI 多過教新同事, 真係好反智. 下一步: 交一件老細睇得明、可以重用嘅 output.
